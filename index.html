Techniques & Simulations

This project explores different animation and simulation techniques in Autodesk Maya, including nCloth, Fluid, nHair and solid/soft body physics.

01 · nCloth

Used to explore cloth simulation and movement within the animated scene.

02 · Fluid

Used to create magical smoke and fluid effects around the cauldron.

03 · nHair

Explored through hair and dynamic simulation experiments. Some tests encountered technical challenges during playback.

04 · Solid / Soft Body Physics

Used to explore physical interactions and dynamic movement between objects.

Production notes: The animation was created in Autodesk Maya and presented as a Playblast. The still images were rendered using Arnold. Additional learning resources included course lectures, YouTube tutorials and ChatGPT for troubleshooting and selected Python scripts. Some 3D assets, including the wizard and room, were downloaded from CGTrader.
