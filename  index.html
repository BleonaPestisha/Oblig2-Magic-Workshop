03 / Behind the Magic

Techniques & Simulations

The project explores four physics and simulation techniques in Autodesk Maya.

01 · nCloth

nCloth is used for simulating flexible fabric and cloth-like surfaces. It allows materials to respond to gravity, movement and collisions.

02 · Fluid Simulation

Maya Fluids is used to create magical smoke and atmospheric effects around the potion, bringing movement and energy into the scene.

03 · nHair

nHair provides dynamic simulation for hair and strand-based elements, allowing them to react to motion and physical forces.

04 · Solid / Soft Body Physics

Solid and soft body physics simulate how objects move, collide and react to forces in the environment.
